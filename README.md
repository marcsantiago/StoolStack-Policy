# StoolStack Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 26, 2026

StoolStack does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal or health data. We do not operate external servers, use third-party analytics, or have access to any information you enter into the app.

### Data Storage & Syncing

All logs, tags, and notes you generate are stored locally on your device. If you enable iCloud, your data is synced across your personal devices using your private Apple iCloud account. Data transferred between your iPhone and Apple Watch occurs directly between your paired devices.

### Apple Health (HealthKit)

If you grant StoolStack permission to read or write data to Apple Health (HealthKit), that information is used solely to display and record your health logs within the app. In compliance with Apple’s guidelines, HealthKit data is never shared with third parties, sold to data brokers, or used for advertising or marketing purposes.

### Data Retention & Deletion

Because we do not store your data on external servers, you remain in full control of your information. You can delete individual logs within the app at any time, or remove all local and iCloud data by uninstalling the app and managing your storage in your device's iOS Settings.

### Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated occasionally. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
